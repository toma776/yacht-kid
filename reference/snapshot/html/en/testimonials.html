Cosmin Mihaila

I have been an owner at Yacht Kid for a month and I am still an owner, the investment already proving its value, showing a real opportunity for a net profit of around €60,000, based on an existing potential buyer.

Yacht Kid is one of the most premium residential developments in Bucharest, perfectly positioned on the lakefront in the Floreasca area.

The project offers an authentic resort lifestyle, with four private outdoor pools overlooking the lake, concierge services including room delivery, a premium reception area, 24/7 security, and a hotel-like experience, with four elevators per floor and fully monitored indoor and outdoor common areas.

The building clearly stands out for the exceptional quality of its construction. Each floor is built using high-grade structural slabs, complemented by triple-glazed French windows, excellent thermal and acoustic insulation, heat pumps, gas heating for cost efficiency, a Class A energy rating, Barisol ceilings, granite bathrooms, and smart rooftop bird deterrent systems. Even the north-facing apartments maintain remarkable comfort and efficiency. All finishes and materials are truly premium.

On the ground floor, residents benefit from a hair salon offering eyebrow shaping, manicures, and men’s haircuts, the Fabriq specialty coffee shop, the Froo supermarket, and many other services currently under development. These include fashion boutiques, wellness services, Pilates studios, tanning centers, and much more. The area is undergoing rapid transformation, with new public schools and kindergartens planned by Sector 2, the Aviation Museum currently under renovation, a hotel under construction across the street, and a strong IT hub nearby.

The location is exceptional, offering direct lake views and soon direct access to Tei Park, proximity to the promenade, spa centers, restaurants, an ice rink, fishing areas, and even Herăstrău Park. The property is located just 500 meters from the Pipera metro station and public transport, with malls, Kaufland, and major retailers nearby.

From an investment perspective, Yacht Kid represents a remarkable opportunity. It is ideal for both long-term rentals and short hotel-style stays, especially for IT professionals and business visitors. Long-term rental values reach approximately 1,200 euros per month for a one-bedroom apartment, while property values have already seen significant growth.

Overall, Yacht Kid is not just a residence, but a strategic premium investment in one of the most exclusive and rapidly developing areas of Bucharest.
