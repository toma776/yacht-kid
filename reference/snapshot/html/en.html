About YACHT KID

Introducing YACHT KID, an exclusive luxury residential development designed to deliver both exceptional living and strong long-term investment value. Strategically positioned in a prime lakeside location, just steps from the water and directly overlooking a future 10,000 sqm park with ample green spaces and dedicated playgrounds, the development benefits from premium positioning and sustained demand potential.

YACHT KID offers high-end apartments, including exclusive residences with private pools, built using advanced technologies and top-quality materials inspired by the yachting industry. Residents enjoy a fully integrated lifestyle ecosystem, featuring a ground-floor café with in-apartment delivery, a grocery store, a premium beauty salon, an upcoming top dental clinic, and a future hot pilates studio, all designed for maximum convenience and everyday comfort.

Awarded at five major international competitions and built to the highest construction standards, YACHT KID stands out through its unique balance of luxury and cost efficiency. Despite its high-end positioning, the development offers some of the lowest maintenance costs on the Bucharest market, starting from only 6 RON/sqm, ensuring optimized post-purchase expenses and strong investment performance.

Every aspect of YACHT KID reflects an uncompromising commitment to quality, intelligent design, and a superior living experience, delivering a top-tier luxury product unlike any other on the market.
